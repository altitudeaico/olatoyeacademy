# Evidence Baseline (condensed synthesis)

*The one-page control on the whole corpus. The full version is the standalone Evidence Baseline Word document; this is the queryable summary.*

## The one headline
The strongest lever in this whole plan is not more academics — it is protecting warmth, play and the girls' own motivation while we stretch them. That is the principle the research most consistently vindicates.

## What is STRONG (build on these)
- **Mastery learning + one-to-one feedback** (Bloom / VanLehn) — our structural superpower.
- **Retrieval practice, spacing, interleaving** (Roediger & Karpicke) — among the most replicated findings in education.
- **Self-Determination Theory** (Deci & Ryan) — autonomy, competence, relatedness; the motivational engine.
- **Vygotsky's ZPD + scaffolding** — the tutoring parent's most practical tool.
- **SEL when SAFE** (Durlak) — 11-percentile-point achievement gain.
- **Ethnic-racial socialization** (Hughes; Wang) — teaching heritage is *protective*, not optional.
- **Acceleration for a ready, willing child** — safe and effective (the good route to "ahead").
- **Attachment as a secure base; executive function is trainable.**

## What is MODERATE (use with caveats)
- Systematic phonics (moderate effect; superiority over other structured methods contested).
- Montessori (for high-fidelity implementations).
- Physical activity & martial arts for self-regulation (small samples).
- Money habits by 7 — but it's *habits of mind*, not facts, that matter.
- Music & executive function.

## What is CONTESTED / small (light framing only — never a pillar)
- **Growth mindset** (Dweck) — near-zero effect on achievement in the best studies (d~0.02).
- **Grit** (Duckworth) — largely redundant with conscientiousness.
- Stages of faith (Fowler/Westerhoff) — descriptive theory, not prescription.

## What is MYTH or WEAK (do not build on)
- **"10,000 hours" as a rule** — it was deliberate *practice quality*, not hours.
- **Music → maths/IQ "far transfer"** — not supported.
- Rigid ages-and-stages formulas (Piaget/Sayers read too literally).
- "Learning styles."
- Home-ed outcome claims that assume causation (self-selected samples). The one robust signal: **structured beats unstructured.**

## The central tension (the guardrail)
**Acceleration (child-led, eager, warm) is safe and effective. Hothousing (adult-pushed, pressured, especially in a young child) is the documented harm** — anxiety, lost curiosity, perfectionism. The "two years ahead" goal is a target for *pace and provision*, never a pressure placed on the child.

### Warning signs that should change the plan
Anxiety, loss of curiosity, perfectionism, tears over work, sleep/appetite changes → immediately reduce pace, increase autonomy and play, re-warm the relationship. This is the single most important dial to watch, especially for the girls.
