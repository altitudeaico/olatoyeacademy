# Reggio Emilia (Malaguzzi)

**Domain:** Home Education Philosophy  |  **Type:** philosophy  |  **Evidence strength:** MODERATE  |  **Era:** 1945-present

## Core idea
Children are capable, curious protagonists who learn through child-led projects and many forms of expression.

## What they teach
Emergent, project-based learning driven by children's interests; the environment as 'third teacher'; documentation of learning; creativity across many media.

## What they would endorse for Olatoye Academy
Excellent for early years and for the creativity pillar - lets Emma's interests drive deep projects.

## What they would caution against
Highly emergent and resource-intensive; needs adult skill to keep it purposeful rather than aimless.

## Evidence strength (read honestly)
MODERATE / well-regarded for early years; evidence base is largely qualitative.

## For Elsie (Year 5 / KS2)
Project-based enrichment tied to her passions (art, chosen topics).

## For Emma (Reception / EYFS)
A natural fit: follow her interests into rich, documented projects; creativity across media.

## Pressure-test prompts
- Whose interest is driving this project - the child's or ours?
- Is the environment inviting exploration?

## Key works
- Edwards, Gandini & Forman, The Hundred Languages of Children
