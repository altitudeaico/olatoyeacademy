# Godly Play (Jerome Berryman)

**Domain:** Christian Faith Formation  |  **Type:** framework  |  **Evidence strength:** FRAMEWORK  |  **Era:** 1990s-present

## Core idea
A Montessori-based, storytelling-and-'wondering' approach to faith for ages 3-12.

## What they teach
Tell scripture stories with simple materials, then invite open 'wondering' questions rather than closed right-answers; let children encounter the story imaginatively.

## What they would endorse for Olatoye Academy
Excellent for Emma's imaginative, sensory faith; brought to the UK by Rebecca Nye. Anchors the early-years side of the Faith pillar.

## What they would caution against
It is formational, not merely informational - resist turning 'wondering' into a quiz. Pair with more structured content as children grow.

## Evidence strength (read honestly)
FRAMEWORK / well-regarded approach; qualitative support.

## For Elsie (Year 5 / KS2)
Wondering can still enrich her, but pair with catechism and the fuller biblical arc.

## For Emma (Reception / EYFS)
Core approach: story + materials + open wondering questions; imaginative encounter with scripture.

## Pressure-test prompts
- Are we inviting genuine wondering or fishing for a 'right' answer?
- Is Emma encountering the story, not just hearing facts?
