# Waldorf / Steiner

**Domain:** Home Education Philosophy  |  **Type:** philosophy  |  **Evidence strength:** CONTESTED  |  **Era:** 1919-present

## Core idea
Education follows developmental rhythms with delayed formal academics and strong arts, imagination and nature.

## What they teach
Delay formal reading/academics; emphasise imaginative play, arts, rhythm and nature in early years.

## What they would endorse for Olatoye Academy
Some early-years instincts (play, rhythm, nature, unhurried early academics) are worth borrowing selectively.

## What they would caution against
IMPORTANT for us: a more contested evidence base, and an underlying metaphysics (anthroposophy) that as Christian parents you should evaluate carefully before adopting wholesale. Borrow practices, not the worldview.

## Evidence strength (read honestly)
CONTESTED - weaker evidence base and a specific spiritual philosophy attached. Adopt selectively and consciously.

## For Elsie (Year 5 / KS2)
Limited relevance; perhaps arts/rhythm elements only.

## For Emma (Reception / EYFS)
Selective borrowing: unhurried early academics, rhythm, nature, imaginative play - without the anthroposophy.

## Pressure-test prompts
- Are we borrowing a Waldorf practice or importing its worldview?
- Does delaying this formal skill serve Emma or just the method?
