# Stages of Faith (Fowler & Westerhoff)

**Domain:** Christian Faith Formation  |  **Type:** framework  |  **Evidence strength:** CONTESTED  |  **Era:** 1976-present

## Core idea
Faith develops through describable stages/styles over a lifetime.

## What they teach
Westerhoff: experienced -> affiliative -> searching -> owned faith. Fowler: structural stages of meaning-making. Children are typically in experienced/affiliative (belonging, shared story) stages.

## What they would endorse for Olatoye Academy
Helps set realistic expectations - young children belong and experience faith before they own or question it.

## What they would caution against
Hold as DESCRIPTIVE theory, not prescription; Fowler in particular is critiqued theologically. Don't force a child toward 'owned faith' prematurely.

## Evidence strength (read honestly)
CONTESTED / descriptive - respected but debated; use as a lens, not a ladder to push children up.

## For Elsie (Year 5 / KS2)
Entering the searching phase is healthy - make room for honest 'why' questions.

## For Emma (Reception / EYFS)
Experienced/affiliative faith: belonging, story, ritual, warmth - not doctrine tests.

## Pressure-test prompts
- Are we honouring where she is in faith, or forcing a later stage?
- Is questioning being welcomed as healthy searching?

## Key works
- Westerhoff, Will Our Children Have Faith?
- Fowler, Stages of Faith (read critically)
