# Music & Cognition

**Domain:** Music Education  |  **Type:** empirical  |  **Evidence strength:** MODERATE  |  **Era:** present

## Core idea
Music training is linked to executive function - but does NOT reliably raise maths or IQ.

## What they teach
Learning an instrument is associated with inhibitory control, working memory and flexibility (inhibition most reliably). But rigorous work finds little 'far transfer' to maths/IQ.

## What they would endorse for Olatoye Academy
Justifies the Music pillar for the RIGHT reasons: focus, discipline, self-regulation and musicianship itself.

## What they would caution against
Do NOT justify music as 'makes you smarter' or a maths booster - that far-transfer claim is not supported. Value it on its own terms.

## Evidence strength (read honestly)
MODERATE for EF links; the 'music -> IQ/maths' transfer is effectively a MYTH (Sala & Gobet).

## For Elsie (Year 5 / KS2)
Formal tuition plus genuine deliberate practice; value the focus and discipline, not a promised maths boost.

## For Emma (Reception / EYFS)
Playful early musicianship for joy, listening and self-regulation.

## Pressure-test prompts
- Are we justifying music honestly (discipline/joy) or overselling a maths boost?
- Is her practice deliberate, or just time at the instrument?
