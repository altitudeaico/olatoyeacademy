# Catechesis & Scripture Memory

**Domain:** Christian Faith Formation  |  **Type:** framework  |  **Evidence strength:** FRAMEWORK  |  **Era:** historic-present

## Core idea
Structured, age-appropriate transmission of biblical content: catechism, scripture memory, and narrative Bible.

## What they teach
Rooted in Deuteronomy 6 (teach diligently to your children). A progression: story + verse for the young; catechism + whole-Bible arc for the older.

## What they would endorse for Olatoye Academy
Provides the 'strong Bible knowledge' backbone Bolaji wants - the structured, measurable content side of the Faith pillar.

## What they would caution against
Memory and catechism must feed formation, not replace it - pair content with relationship and wonder (see Nye, Godly Play).

## Evidence strength (read honestly)
FRAMEWORK / historic mainstream practice across traditions.

## For Elsie (Year 5 / KS2)
Longer memory passages, catechism Q&A, the whole arc of scripture, honest 'why' discussion.

## For Emma (Reception / EYFS)
Short verses, vivid stories, songs, a narrative story-Bible; wondering questions.

## Pressure-test prompts
- Is memory work feeding formation or becoming rote-only?
- Do we have a real progression of Bible content per child?
