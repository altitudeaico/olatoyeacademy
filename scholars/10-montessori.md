# Maria Montessori

**Domain:** Home Education Philosophy  |  **Type:** philosophy  |  **Evidence strength:** MODERATE  |  **Era:** 1870-1952

## Core idea
The environment itself teaches; children learn through self-directed activity in a carefully prepared space.

## What they teach
Prepared environment, sensitive periods (developmental windows), child-led choice within limits, practical-life activities, concrete hands-on materials, mixed-age learning.

## What they would endorse for Olatoye Academy
A strong fit for Emma. Justifies our intentionality about learning spaces and not defaulting to whatever is most comfortable.

## What they would caution against
Fidelity matters - benefits appear for high-fidelity implementations, not diluted 'Montessori-ish' setups. Child-led does not mean unstructured.

## Evidence strength (read honestly)
MODERATE - generally positive for high-fidelity implementations; mixed overall. Public domain writings.

## For Elsie (Year 5 / KS2)
Selected principles: ordered environment, practical independence, concrete materials for new maths concepts.

## For Emma (Reception / EYFS)
Core approach: prepared environment, real practical-life tasks, freedom within clear limits, hands-on materials.

## Pressure-test prompts
- Is Emma's environment prepared to invite concentration, or cluttered?
- Are we offering child-led choice within limits, or just free-for-all?
