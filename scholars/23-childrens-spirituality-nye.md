# Children's Spirituality (Rebecca Nye)

**Domain:** Christian Faith Formation  |  **Type:** framework  |  **Evidence strength:** FRAMEWORK  |  **Era:** 1998-present

## Core idea
Children's spirituality centres on 'relational consciousness' - their innate awareness in relationship to God, others, world and self.

## What they teach
Nurture (don't manufacture) the child's existing spiritual capacity; protect space, imagination, and relationship; principles over programmes.

## What they would endorse for Olatoye Academy
Grounds the Faith pillar in the child's own spiritual life, not just content delivery; accessible home principles.

## What they would caution against
Avoid reducing faith to information or performance; protect unhurried, genuine spiritual space.

## Evidence strength (read honestly)
FRAMEWORK - influential, research-informed (Cambridge); qualitative.

## For Elsie (Year 5 / KS2)
Make room for honest questions and genuine encounter, not just knowledge checks.

## For Emma (Reception / EYFS)
Protect wonder and relationship; nurture the spirituality she already has.

## Pressure-test prompts
- Are we nurturing her spirituality or just delivering content?
- Is there unhurried space for genuine encounter?
