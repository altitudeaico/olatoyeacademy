# Music Pedagogies (Suzuki, Kodaly, Dalcroze, Orff)

**Domain:** Music Education  |  **Type:** framework  |  **Evidence strength:** FRAMEWORK  |  **Era:** 20th c.-present

## Core idea
Established methods for teaching music, each suited to different ages and goals.

## What they teach
Suzuki: ear-before-notation, heavy parental involvement, early start. Kodaly: voice/solfege-based. Dalcroze: music through movement. Orff: percussion and improvisation.

## What they would endorse for Olatoye Academy
Gives a concrete route into the Music pillar: Suzuki fits Emma (early, ear-based, parent-involved); formal tuition + deliberate practice fits Elsie.

## What they would caution against
Suzuki's heavy parental involvement is a real time commitment - go in eyes open. Match method to child and goal.

## Evidence strength (read honestly)
FRAMEWORK / established practice; choice of method is pedagogical, not a strength claim.

## For Elsie (Year 5 / KS2)
Formal instrument tuition combined with short, focused, feedback-rich deliberate practice.

## For Emma (Reception / EYFS)
Suzuki or a Kodaly/Dalcroze-style playful, ear-first start; parent involvement built in.

## Pressure-test prompts
- Does the chosen method fit this child's age and our capacity?
- Are we ready for Suzuki's parental time commitment?
