# Jubilee Centre (Character Education)

**Domain:** Character & Social-Emotional  |  **Type:** framework  |  **Evidence strength:** FRAMEWORK  |  **Era:** 2012-present

## Core idea
Character is built through four types of virtue - intellectual, moral, civic, performance - unified by practical wisdom (phronesis).

## What they teach
Virtues are cultivated through practice, habituation and reflection, guided by phronesis (knowing the right thing to do when virtues conflict). Confidence and poise come from genuine competence plus practice.

## What they would endorse for Olatoye Academy
The UK's leading evidence-based character framework. Grounds the Character/Etiquette/Confidence pillar - and says confidence comes from real competence and practice (e.g. public speaking), not praise.

## What they would caution against
Character cannot be praised into existence or taught only as slogans - it requires real practice and reflection over time.

## Evidence strength (read honestly)
FRAMEWORK - a respected, research-informed model (University of Birmingham) rather than a single effect size.

## For Elsie (Year 5 / KS2)
Build confidence through genuine competence and repeated practice - public speaking, performance, real responsibility.

## For Emma (Reception / EYFS)
Habituate simple virtues (kindness, honesty, courtesy) through daily practice and gentle reflection.

## Pressure-test prompts
- Are we building confidence from real competence, or just praise?
- Which virtue are we actually practising, not just naming?
