# Decision Log

Every meaningful call about the Academy gets recorded here after being pressure-tested against the corpus. Over time this becomes the story of *how Olatoye Academy reasons* — the real governance record.

- Copy `_TEMPLATE.md`, rename to `YYYY-MM-slug.md`, fill it in.
- Always list which scholar lenses you consulted and note their evidence strength.
- Revisit on the review date and update `status`.

**Example decisions to log:** the two-years-ahead target; choosing a debate/public-speaking provider; adding martial arts; Emma's reading pace; a new instrument and method.
